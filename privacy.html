Luma Garden

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 August 2026

The short version. Luma Garden does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. There is no account, no sign-in, no chat, no analytics and no tracking. Your child's progress never leaves their device.

What is stored, and where

The game saves two things in the device's local storage, and only there:

Progress
Which gardens are complete, stars earned, diamonds, hints and bombs.
Settings
Sound, reduce motion, and whether adverts are shown.

Neither is sent anywhere. Uninstalling the app, or using “Reset all progress” in Settings, removes it.

Adverts

Adverts are optional and can be switched off at any time in Settings without losing anything in the game.

When adverts are on, they are served by Google AdMob and are configured as non-personalised: they are not based on your child's interests and are not used to build a profile. The app is marked as child-directed, so Google restricts what may be shown. AdMob may process a device identifier in order to serve and measure an advert and to prevent fraud. Google's practices are described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Switching adverts off, or buying any supporter pack, stops them entirely.

The weekly league

If you play a league garden, the game sends that score to a leaderboard along with two things: a nickname the game picked from a fixed word list, and a random identifier the app generated for itself. Your child never types a name. That identifier is not a device or advertising ID — it identifies nothing but a copy of the game, and clearing the app's storage discards it.

There is no profile, no messaging, and no way for players to contact each other. Scores older than the current week are not shown.

Purchases

Garden packs are handled entirely by Apple or Google. We never see or store your payment details. A purchase records two things on the device — that adverts are off, and the diamonds it added — and nothing else. Nothing about a purchase is sent to us.

Children

This game is made for children. It contains no advertising that profiles a child, no external links in the play area, no social features, and no way for a child to contact anyone. Every purchase is behind a grown-up check.

We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the UK Age Appropriate Design Code, and the Google Play Families policy.

Your rights

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to look up, export or delete. Everything the game knows lives on the device and is under your control.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it.
