Wake the Garden

Some gardens are still asleep.

Luma is a small light who lives at the edge of the garden. Turn the stone paths so her glow can travel, around corners, past frozen stones, through thorns, until every flower wakes up.

It is a quiet game. Most gardens have no timer, there is nothing to lose, and you cannot get stuck. There are 500 of them, and they grow slowly, from four stones at the start to thirty near the end.

If your child gets stuck, the game will help. A hint lights up one useful stone, and nothing is ever wasted by getting it wrong.

Free, and staying that way

No paywall. No waiting timers. Not one garden hidden behind a purchase, and nothing at all to buy.

There is one small advert below the garden. It never appears during play, and a grown-up can switch it off in Settings without losing anything.

Made for children, and built that way

No account. No sign-in. No chat, and no way for anyone to contact your child. They never type their name anywhere, so there is nothing for us to moderate and nothing personal to leak.

Progress stays on the device. The full detail is in the privacy policy, which is short and written in plain English.

The weekly league

One garden each week is the same puzzle for everybody. Hints and bombs are switched off there, so it comes down to the solving alone. Players appear under a nickname the game picks for them, never one they type.
