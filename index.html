Luma Garden

Some gardens are still asleep.

Luma is a small light who lives at the edge of the garden. Turn the stone paths so her glow can travel — around corners, past frozen stones, through thorns — until every flower wakes up.

Luma Garden is a quiet puzzle game for curious minds. There is no timer on most gardens, nothing to lose, and no way to get stuck. 500 gardens grow slowly from four stones to thirty, across twenty chapters.

Free, and staying that way

No paywall, no waiting timers, and not one garden hidden behind a purchase. An optional supporter pack removes the adverts for good, and that is the only thing it does. Every player gets the same 500 gardens either way.

Made for children, and built that way

No account, no sign-in, no chat, and no way for a player to contact anyone. Your child never types their name. Progress stays on the device, and adverts can be switched off in Settings at any time without losing anything.

The full detail is in the privacy policy.
