Luma Garden

Why I made this

Luma Garden is free, and it always will be. This page explains why it exists. Nothing here changes the game — every player gets the same 500 gardens whether they ever give a penny or not.

My son

My son has Duchenne muscular dystrophy.

Duchenne is a genetic condition. The body carries an error in the gene that makes dystrophin, a protein muscles need in order to repair themselves after ordinary use. Without enough of it, muscle is slowly lost and replaced. It affects walking first, and in time it reaches the muscles that matter most.

In his case the error is a pathogenic point mutation in exon 70 — a single misspelled letter, in one section of one gene, out of everything that makes him who he is. He is a child who laughs at his own jokes before he finishes telling them. The rest of him is perfectly, ordinarily fine.

What we are hoping for

There is now a one-time gene therapy for Duchenne called Elevidys (delandistrogene moxeparvovec). It delivers a shortened, working copy of the dystrophin gene into muscle, so the body can make a version of the protein it cannot currently produce.

It is not a cure, and I want to be honest about that. It is not expected to undo what has already happened. What his doctors hope is that it slows down what happens next — that it buys him years of walking, of climbing stairs on his own, of being a boy rather than a patient.

With Duchenne, time is the whole thing. Muscle that is lost does not come back. Every month that passes before treatment is a month that cannot be recovered.

The cost

Elevidys carries a list price of approximately US$3.2 million for a single dose. It is given once.

That is not a number a family reaches by saving. It is why I am asking at all.

Where the game comes in

I am a software developer. Making a game is the one useful thing I know how to do that does not take time away from him — most of Luma Garden was built late at night, after bedtime.

The game earns in two small ways: adverts, which any player can switch off, and optional supporter packs, which remove the adverts for good and grant nothing else. It is a real game, sold honestly, and I would want people to enjoy it even if none of the rest of this were true.

It will not raise three million dollars. I know that. It is simply the part I can do myself, instead of only waiting.
